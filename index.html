FROM A LARGE PLAYER POOL TO A REVIEWABLE QUESTION

Make the shortlist explainable.

A scout needs context, not another opaque ranking. Touchline connects identity, exposure and role-relative statistics so an analyst can show why a player deserves a closer look.

01 / DISCOVER

Find a profile

Scout Board ranks within roles, with deterministic ties and explicit eligibility. Search by player, club or ID; filter league, broad role and minutes. Inspect how each weighted metric contributes to a descriptive screening score.

02 / COMPARE

Look beyond the headline

Compare two or three Player Intelligence profiles, find Similar Profiles, and build a session-only shortlist with review notes. Signed feature contributions explain what drives similarity.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Challenge the estimate

Read saved next-season estimates beside a simple baseline, empirical bands and observed-outcome coverage. Ask what the numbers leave out.

CASE 01 / CREATIVE PROFILES

Who shares Michael Olise’s observed profile?

Bayern, 2024/25 · AM/W · 2,486 expanded minutes. His descriptive screening score is 85.20/100 across a 356-record role pool. That is a weighted heuristic, not a talent forecast.

Same-role, same-season comparables suggest a video-review queue. Check receiving zones, pressure, off-ball work and team context before interpreting the match.

CASE 02 / DOES COMPLEXITY HELP?

Compare ML with a strong simple baseline.

Ridge’s documented error is lower than the shrinkage baseline on 391 observed outcomes. The absolute MAE difference is 0.008685363 per expanded 90. The comparison is more informative than presenting an ML score in isolation.

133 candidates lack usable outcomes. Their absence can select for participation and opportunity; they cannot be assigned zero output.

ENGINEERING / TRACEABILITY

An inspectable chain of evidence.

  1. Original release
    SHA-256 verification
  2. Identity & quality
    coverage, minutes, stints
  3. Features & models
    role-relative, chronological
  4. Saved evidence
    manifests and reports
  5. Human review
    video and context

Python · pandas · NumPy · PyArrow · scikit-learn · SciPy · Streamlit · Plotly · pytest. No paid data API, synthetic production fallback or live model retraining.

Identity before inference

Player IDs keep namesakes separate. Age and nationality are unverified. Covered team stints combine before research rates are calculated. Historical source ages do not establish U23 eligibility.

Quality stays visible

Missing matches, source issues, expanded minutes and missing outcomes remain part of the interpretation. No missing statistic is invented. The audit excludes 124 corrupted final-third-pass rates and 1,315 completed-pass rates. All 347 CM/DM scores are withheld; 1,184 records remain score-eligible.

Claims stay bounded

No claims about market value, contracts, injuries, representation, transfer availability, potential or causal club fit. Independent research project.
